OUR STORY

s3 Fitness, brought to life by founder Sudhir & Subhash’s vision that a healthy lifestyle elevates every aspect of life, is now open in Badli and ready to welcome you. Imagine a classy, private sanctuary, where every workout is guided by professionals who bring expert care, genuine warmth, and joyful dedication. Whether you’re stepping in for the first time or aiming for the next level, here you’ll find focused training, elegant ambiance, and a team eager to support your wellness journey. Experience fitness redefined—one private session, personalized goal, and confident stride at a time.

OUR ADVANTAGES

Qualified trainers

You’ve likely found yourself inspired by the way a skilled trainer guides a class and captures attention. Along the way, you may have picked up on the qualities that distinguish exceptional trainers and formed your own idea of what makes them truly effective.

Individual training

Personal trainers are qualified fitness professionals who specialize in creating and supervising exercise programs. Their role goes beyond instruction—they encourage clients, track progress, and ensure accountability to help them reach their health and fitness goals.

Multiple classis

Our clients can choose from multiple engaging classes such as Zumba, Aerobics, Kickboxing, Strength, Power Yoga, and Free Weight training.

Hightech gym

During every workout session, performance is tracked using the latest technology, including wearables, cardio devices, and projection screens. This ensures accuracy, motivation, and a more engaging fitness experience.

Men and Women's changing rooms

Enjoy dedicated, secure changing spaces designed for both comfort and privacy. With luxurious locker facilities, soft lighting, and a clean, serene ambiance, our changing rooms let you refresh and recharge before and after your workout.

Free Wi-Fi Zone

Stay connected at every step. Enjoy fast, complimentary Wi-Fi in all member areas— perfect for streaming workouts, catching up on messages, or tracking your fitness goals.
