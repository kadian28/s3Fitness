Classes The Gym

Your body is your lasting foundation—build it with care.

GYM FITNESS

Exercise and recovery are the foundations of a healthier life. It’s not always easy, but the results are always worth it. Once you discover a fitness routine you enjoy, you’ll look better, feel stronger, and gain more confidence.

INDOOR CYCLING

Cycling is not only an excellent workout but also a fun, practical way to stay active. In our gym, we bring the benefits of cycling indoors with stationary bikes and weighted flywheels, offering a safe and effective class experience without the risks of outdoor riding.

POWER YOGA

Tone up, slim down, and find balance with Power Yoga—a high-energy workout that combines strength, flexibility, and mindfulness. Beyond the physical benefits, it brings inner peace, a spiritual lift, and a deep sense of serenity.

CARDIO FITNESS

Cardio fitness is essential for everyone. High-quality cardio workouts can help you achieve a wide range of health and fitness goals—from improving athletic performance to lowering blood pressure, reducing cholesterol, and managing diabetes.

CARDIO KICK BOXING

Kickboxing is more than just a great way to get fit—it helps you build strength, stamina, and confidence. Popular among fitness enthusiasts, it’s an exciting alternative to traditional workouts that keeps things fresh and engaging..

AEROBICS CLASSES

Aerobic exercises—such as walking, running, and swimming—are among the most effective ways to burn fat and lose weight. Research shows that aerobics is one of the most efficient methods for reducing body fat and improving overall fitness.

CIRCUIT TRAINING

Circuit training is a dynamic, fast-paced workout where you move through a series of exercises, each lasting from 30 seconds to 5 minutes. This method boosts strength, improves aerobic fitness, and burns plenty of calories, all while keeping you engaged and energized.

FREE WEIGHTS FLOOR

Our gym features a complete range of free weights and benches, including dumbbells, Olympic lifting platforms, plate-loaded strength machines, power cages, kettlebells, and gymnastic rings—everything you need to train with precision and power.

STRENGTH CLASSES

Looking to build strength and boost both aerobic and anaerobic fitness? Our conditioning classes are designed to challenge your body and mind. Strength training isn’t just about muscle mass—it’s about building endurance, improving performance, and supporting overall health.

OUTDOOR TRAINING

Our Outdoor Training sessions—featuring Running Club and Park Fitness—are designed for all fitness levels and abilities. Training outdoors is one of the most effective ways to burn fat while enjoying fresh air and the beauty of the city.
