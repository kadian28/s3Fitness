Our Story

Founded by Sudhir & Subhash Dhankhar, S3 Fitness was built on the belief that a healthy lifestyle leads to a better quality of life.
What began as a vision to create private, world-class fitness spaces has grown into one of Delhi’s most modern and trusted fitness club brands.

In 2016, driven by a shared vision of elevating wellness, Subhash & Sudhir took a bold step and launched S3 Fitness Gym in Badli. What started as a focused endeavor evolved into a sanctuary of strength, elegance, and community.

Nestled in the heart of Badli, S3 Fitness Gym quickly made its mark by blending advanced training methodologies with a welcoming, personalized atmosphere. Subhash’s unwavering dedication to technical discipline and mindful instruction ensured that each workout felt purposeful and effective.

Over the years, S3 expanded its offerings to include a comprehensive suite of programs—strength conditioning, functional training, specialized classes, and one-on-one coaching—designed to meet members exactly where they were in their fitness journeys.

The training space, both polished and inviting, cultivated an environment where members felt seen, supported, and motivated—whether they were pushing through their first push-up or aiming for elite performance.

Subhash fostered a team of highly trained professionals who matched his passion for member-focused care, ensuring that every training session offered more than just physical results—it offered confidence, balance, and renewal.

Today, S3 Fitness Gym in Badli stands as a hallmark of holistic fitness—a place where each rep, each stretch, and each interaction contributes to a stronger body, a sharper mind, and a tighter-knit fitness community.

Subhash & Sudhir’s vision remains the beating heart of the gym: to empower, elevate, and inspire—one member at a time.
